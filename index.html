The Montessori Method

What is the Montessori Methood?

The Montessori method is a form of education involving hands on learning, self-directed activity and collaborative play with an emphasis of encouraging independence.Unlike traditional style education, Montessori style allows children to independently learn at their own pace with help of guidance.

Who created the Montessori Method ?

Dr.Maria Montessori was an Italian educator, physican and scientist who developed this approach of education. She was the first to open a Montessori school in Rome in 1907 paving the way for thousands of schools worldwide now. She started this journey in a psychiatric clinic working with children that were categorized "unteachable". She introduces hand on activities and let them explore freely. Nevertheless her finding and observations showed otherwise using this approach of education inspiring her to continue to learn how children learn best.

Problem Solving Skills

One of the main emphasis in this approach is to give the child freedom to be curious and find answers from within. Parents/teachers/caregivers are not to give solutions to the childs problem but rather guide them to be able to resolve their problem. When this situation happens you would usually ask questions that lead to thoughtful thinking from the child. Overall, the child continues to critically think and solve helping them achieve greater things further on in their future.

Confidence & Self-esteem

This education approach encourages children to go at their own pace and set their own goal. When you allow your child from a young age make their own choice and act on it they gain confidence in what they do. With this style of learing there is only positive reinforcment and no competetion with other kids. This allows the child to develop self worth and self-esteem that will allow them to be confident and true to themselves in the future.

Practical Life Skills

Most activities that are implemented in this method of learning is to allow children from a young age to do things such as pouring water in a cup, putting their own clothes, cutting their own fruit etc.. This causes a child to be more in tune with the enviroment creating that relationship of value. This overall allows the child to gain confidence and continue to handle personal duties at home and in school.

Independence & Self-direct learning

One of the main emphasis in Montessori is that every child is able to grow and learn. Many of the classrooms/house setting is having things at a chhilds reach so they are able to grab or try anyting by themselves instead of having to ask. They are free to decide what they want to do and learn at their own pace. These activites are hands on meaning they do it themselves with guidance if necessary. Overall this shows the child they are capable of doing things themselves and allows them to continue being independent ain the future.
